THE DESTINATION

Welcome to the Mystic Island.

Siquijor is an island province in the Central Visayas area of the Philippines, celebrated for waterfalls, marine life, coastal scenery, and cultural heritage. Stories of traditional healing are also part of its distinctive local identity.

Its six municipalities are Siquijor, San Juan, Lazi, Maria, Enrique Villanueva, and Larena. Visitors can explore a blend of seaside towns, inland landscapes, and local communities.

A LIVING HERITAGE

History you can experience.

Siquijor’s communities preserve historic churches, colonial-era architecture, and living cultural traditions. Lazi’s church and convent are well-known heritage landmarks, while local folklore forms part of the island’s story.

The island’s tourism appeal is its balance: gentle beach days, waterfall adventures, underwater exploration, cultural learning, and slow travel.
