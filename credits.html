Travel notice, privacy & accuracy

Isla Siquijor is an independent travel-guide website and is not an official tourism department or booking company. Sample itineraries and estimated costs are planning references only. Named accommodations and attractions are included for trip research and are not presented as affiliated partners.

Inquiry form data remains in your browser until you choose to send an email from your own email application; this site has no database, analytics, or form-processing server. External sites and embedded maps follow their own privacy policies. The contact inbox in the code is a configurable placeholder and must be replaced before live use.
