GETTING THERE

The journey to Siquijor.

Siquijor is primarily reached by sea. Always verify routes and ferry timetables with the operator before departing.

01

Travel to a gateway

Common travel routes connect through Dumaguete, Cebu, or Bohol, depending on where you begin.

02

Board a ferry

Check the operator, departure port, terminal requirements, and the exact arrival port on your ticket.

03

Explore locally

Arrange a licensed driver, tricycle, or other suitable transport. Use caution with motorbike rentals.

⛴ Travel reminder: Routes, fares, schedules, and travel advisories can change. Confirm directly with the ferry operator and official provincial tourism channels.
PACK SMART

Good things to know.

☀

Best time & weather

Many travelers prefer the generally drier months, but rain and sea conditions are unpredictable. Check forecasts and ferry advisories.

🎒

What to bring

Quick-dry clothes, sunscreen, water bottle, sturdy footwear, cash, an eco-friendly dry bag, and basic personal medication.

👕

What to wear

Comfortable tropical clothing for sightseeing; modest clothing in churches and appropriate shoes on trails and caves.

♻

Travel responsibly

Do not touch coral or wildlife, take litter home, follow marked paths, and honor sanctuary rules.

♡

Local customs

Ask before photographing people or ceremonies. Treat folk traditions with sensitivity and respect.

♿

Accessibility

Stairs, uneven trails, and rocky access may limit some locations. Contact attractions and accommodation providers for specific needs.

₱

Budget planning

Account for ferries, lodging, meals, transport, guides, snorkeling equipment, and entrance fees. Keep a little buffer.

☎

Emergency help

For urgent emergencies in the Philippines, call 911 . Ask your accommodation for the closest medical facility.

FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Questions before you go.

How many days should I stay? +

Three days makes a comfortable first introduction. If you want guided caving, long snorkeling sessions, or extra rest, consider adding time.

Where should I stay? +

San Juan is convenient for beach sunsets and marine activities. Lazi suits southern waterfall and heritage-focused travelers. Other towns may be quieter.

Can I reserve directly through this website? +

No. The itineraries are planning suggestions. Confirm availability, final pricing, reservations, and payments directly with your chosen local provider.

Is Siquijor safe to explore? +

As with any destination, follow local safety notices, take care on roads and trails, use qualified guides for caves, and avoid unsafe sea conditions.

Are the suggested budgets exact? +

No. All budgets are indicative planning ranges, not published operator quotations. Confirm current prices locally.
